## What credits are

Credits are how you pay on Orochia. One credit is one US cent, so 100 credits are one dollar. Your balance is shown in dollars.

## Add credits

1. Open your wallet on orochia.com and choose to add credits.
2. Pick a pack.
3. Pay on the secure checkout page of the payment provider.

Card details are typed on the provider's page and never reach Orochia.

| You pay | You get |
| :--- | :--- |
| $10 | $10 in credits |
| $25 | $26 in credits |
| $50 | $54 in credits |
| $100 | $110 in credits |

Bigger packs include bonus credits. The app shows your balance and sends you to the website to add more.

## What you can do with credits

- **Unlock a video.** The creator sets a minimum amount. The page shows it. After you pay, the unlock is yours to keep. You can pay more as a tip.
- **Send a tip.** A tip goes straight to the creator.
- **Bid in an auction.** See [Auctions](/docs/auctions).
- **Back a challenge.** See [Challenges](/docs/challenges).

## Held credits

When you bid or pledge, the credits are held, not spent. Your wallet shows the balance and what is held. Held credits are released if you are outbid, if the auction or challenge is declined, cancelled or not delivered. They are spent only when you win or when the creator delivers.

## Questions

If a payment did not complete, nothing is spent. Read the [FAQ](/docs/faq) or the page on [your account and privacy](/docs/account-and-privacy).
