A challenge is a video that fans ask for and a creator makes. The rule is simple: nobody pays for nothing. Credits are held until the video or story is delivered. If it does not happen, they come back.

## Three kinds

| Kind | Who starts it | What happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Goal | A verified creator | Fans pledge until the deadline. If the goal is reached, the creator makes it. |
| Dare | Any member, for one creator | The creator has 72 hours to accept or decline. |
| Open call | Any member | Creators apply. The author picks one before the deadline. |

A challenge is delivered as a video, which the backers keep, or as a story, which lasts 24 hours. The creator has 1 to 14 days to deliver once they commit.

## Back a challenge

1. Open the **Challenges** page and choose one.
2. See the pot, the time left and the top backers.
3. Choose an amount, from $1. Suggestions are $5, $10, $25 and $50.
4. Choose **Pledge**.

## What happens to your credits

- When you pledge, they are held.
- If the goal is missed, the dare is declined or ignored, nobody is picked, or the video is not delivered, they come back.
- When the creator delivers, they are paid.

You cannot back a challenge you started yourself as a creator, and an applicant to an open call cannot pledge on it.

## Start a dare or an open call

A dare needs an offer of at least the creator's minimum, $10 by default. An open call needs a pot of at least $5. The offer is held at once, and others can add to it. Start one from the Challenges page on orochia.com.

## Privacy

Backers appear as Backer 1, Backer 2 and so on, and you see **You** for yours. The member who sent a dare is visible only to the creator it was sent to.

## Creators

Only verified creators set goals, accept dares and deliver. In **Settings, Challenges**, a creator can turn dares off or set their minimum. They can decline anything they do not want to make.
