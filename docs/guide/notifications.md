When something happens that concerns you, Orochia tells you. A new follower, a tip, an unlock, a bid, a pledge, a message.

## Where you hear about it

- **On the website.** The bell shows a count, a small message slides in, and the list updates.
- **In the app.** A small message appears while the app is open.
- **On your phone, with the app closed.** A push notification. It works on Android today and will come to iPhone with the iPhone app.
- **By e-mail.** You choose the pace: right away, once an hour, or never.

## Choose what you get

Everything is on at first. You can turn each kind off.

- **On the website,** open **Settings**, then **Notifications**. You can set each event, in the app and by e-mail, and the e-mail pace.
- **In the app,** open **You**, then **Notification settings**. You can turn kinds on or off: messages, followers and contacts, comments, tips and unlocks, videos, auctions, challenges and storage.

The "in the app" choice covers the bell, the small messages and the push together. A push only repeats what the bell shows.

## Messages

A message rings your phone only when none of your Orochia screens is open. If one is open, the message shows there. E-mails about one conversation are limited to one an hour.

## Auctions and challenges

You hear when an auction is announced, when you are outbid, when you win or lose, and at each step of a challenge you backed or made. Each one can be switched off.
