Orochia is a video platform for independent creators. It is for adults only. This page takes you from a first visit to your first video.

## Create your account

There is one kind of account. You join as a member, a fan. If you want to publish later, you open a creator space on the same account. You never sign up twice.

1. Open orochia.com and choose **Sign up**.
2. Give an e-mail address, a username and a password of 10 characters or more.
3. Give your date of birth. Orochia checks that you are 18 or older. It stays private and is never shown.
4. Tick that you are at least 18 and that you accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
5. Open the e-mail we send you and follow the link. Until you do, your account can only confirm the address.

Your username is your address: orochia.com/@yourusername.

## The 18+ gate

Orochia shows an adults-only gate before anything else. Leaving is one tap. Adult videos play only for a signed-in account whose age Orochia has checked from the date of birth. Ticking a box in the browser opens nothing by itself.

## Find creators

- **Explore** lists the creators and videos you can see.
- A creator's page shows their videos, stories and what each one asks of you.
- Follow a creator to see their new videos on your home feed.

## Watch

Each video tells you what it asks. It can be free, for followers, for contacts, for people who unlock it, for invited people, for an auction winner or for the backers of a challenge. Read more in [Watching and following](/docs/watching), and look up any word in the [Vocabulary](/docs/vocabulary).

## Support the creators you like

You can tip, unlock a video, bid in an auction or back a challenge. All of these use Orochia credits. See [Credits, tips and unlocks](/docs/credits).
