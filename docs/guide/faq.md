## Is Orochia free?

Joining is free. Some videos are free to watch. Others ask for an unlock, and auctions and challenges use credits.

## Who can use Orochia?

Adults only: 18 or older. See [Safety and the 18+ rules](/docs/safety).

## Do I need the app?

No. orochia.com works in a phone browser. The app adds stories, notifications and a faster feed. See [The Orochia app](/docs/mobile).

## Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. It comes with TestFlight soon.

## How do I pay?

With credits. You add them on the website, on the payment provider's page. See [Credits, tips and unlocks](/docs/credits).

## Does Orochia see my card?

No. You type it on the payment provider's page.

## What if I lose an auction or a challenge?

Your held credits come back to you. See [Auctions](/docs/auctions) and [Challenges](/docs/challenges).

## Why can I not watch a video?

The page tells you. It can be for followers, for contacts, for people who unlock it, or for invited people. It can also need a signed-in account with a checked age. See [Watching and following](/docs/watching).

## I forgot my password

Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page of orochia.com.

## How do I become a creator?

Open a creator space on your account. See [Become a creator](/docs/creator-onboarding).

## How do I report something?

Choose **Report** on the video or story. See [Safety and the 18+ rules](/docs/safety).
