## What others see

- Your **username** and your public page, orochia.com/@yourusername.
- Your **display name**, picture, cover, bio and links, if you add them. Several people can share a display name. Nobody shares a username.
- Your name only appears in auctions and challenges as an alias, such as Bidder 2 or Backer 3. You see **You** for yours.

## What stays private

- Your **e-mail address.** It is used to sign in and for the notifications you keep.
- Your **date of birth.** Orochia uses it to confirm you are 18 or older. It is never shown.
- Your card details. They are entered on the payment provider's page and never reach Orochia.
- Your payout method, if you are a creator. It is stored encrypted and shown only in part.

## Your controls

Open **Settings**. You will find:

| Section | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| Profile | Name, picture, cover, bio |
| Links | Your website and social links |
| Privacy | Who can send you messages |
| Notifications | What you hear about, and how |
| Payouts | For creators: where your money goes |
| Sign-in methods | How you sign in |
| Blocked accounts | People who cannot message you, follow you or ask to be your contact |
| Challenges | For creators: dares and minimum offers |

## Your data

Read the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for what Orochia keeps and why. The page also says how to ask about your data.

## Sign out on a shared phone

When you sign out of the app, it forgets your session and clears what it stored. The next person on that phone never sees your account.
