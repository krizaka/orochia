---
title: Auctions
description: How to bid on a video, what happens when you are outbid, and who gets the video.
---

A creator can put one of their videos up for auction. People bid with credits. When the auction ends, the highest bidder gets the video, and only them.

## Bid

1. Open the **Auctions** page and choose an auction.
2. See the current bid, the time left and the next minimum bid.
3. Pick an amount and choose **Bid**.
4. You see **You lead** while your bid is the highest.

You need enough credits in your wallet. If you do not, the page says so and shows your balance.

## The rules

- The starting price is at least $1.00.
- An auction lasts from 1 hour to 14 days.
- Each bid must beat the last one by a step: $0.50 under $10, $1 under $50, $5 under $200, $10 under $1,000, then $25.
- If someone bids in the last 2 minutes, the end moves to 2 minutes after that bid. Nobody can win by bidding at the last second.

## Your credits stay safe

- When you bid, your credits are held.
- If someone outbids you, they come back to you at once.
- If you win, they are spent and the video is yours.
- If the auction is declined or cancelled, they come back.

## Who wins

Some creators choose to decide. They have 48 hours to accept or decline the best bid. If they say nothing, the bid is declined and your credits come back. Other auctions sell to the highest bid on their own.

The winner can watch the video, or watch and download it, as the creator chose. The auction page says which.

## Privacy

Bidders appear as Bidder 1, Bidder 2 and so on. You see **You** for your own bids. Only the creator sees the name of the leader, to decide.

## Notifications

You hear when an auction is announced by a creator you follow, when you are outbid, and when you win or lose. You choose which ones in [Notifications](/docs/notifications).
