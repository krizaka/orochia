title: Accessibility
description: What Orochia does so that everyone can use it, and how to tell us when it falls short.
---

Orochia aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, on the website and in the app.

## What we check on every change

- Automatic checks of the main pages of the website, in the dark and the light theme, on a desktop and a phone.
- A keyboard-only check. The first Tab goes to **Skip to content**. Dialogs take the focus when they open and give it back when they close.
- Text colours that stay readable in both themes.
- In the app, every key screen is checked for labels, roles and touch size.

## Things you can do

- Use the website with the keyboard alone.
- Switch between the dark and the light theme.
- Use the app with your phone's screen reader. Buttons and images have names.

## Something blocks you

Tell us in [the accessibility discussion](https://github.com/krizaka/orochia/discussions/133). Say which page and what you use, and we will look at it.
