Orochia uses the same words on the website, in the app, in e-mails and here. This page says what each one means.

## What you watch

### Story

A story is a picture or a short video that **disappears after 24 hours**. Stories are the rings at the top of your home. A ring around a creator's picture means there is a story you have not seen yet. Tap it to open it.

- A video story is vertical (9:16) and lasts up to 60 seconds.
- Its 24 hours start when it is ready to play.
- Who can see it follows the same rules as a video: the creator chooses.
- An image story stays on screen for 5 seconds in the app, then the next one follows.

![A story, full screen, in the app](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/site/docs/orochia/mobile/07-story-dark.a195f81935.webp "phone")

### Short

A short is a **vertical video of 3 minutes or less**. When a video is taller than it is wide (like 9:16 or 4:5) and lasts at most 3 minutes, it goes on the **Shorts** row. Scroll the row sideways, or use the arrows on a computer. A short stays online, it does not disappear.

Orochia sets the limit, and it can change it. A vertical video that is longer than the limit goes in the Videos grid, like any other video.

### Video

A video is every other video: wide (16:9), square, or vertical and longer than a short. Videos are in the grid under the Shorts row, labelled **Videos**. A video stays online until its creator takes it down.

### The difference in one line

| | Story | Short | Video |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shape | Vertical | Vertical, 3 minutes or less | Anything else |
| How long it stays | 24 hours | Until the creator removes it | Until the creator removes it |
| Where you find it | The rings at the top | The Shorts row | The Videos grid |

## Who can watch

Every video, short and story shows what it asks. The label appears on the card and on the page.

| Word | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| Free | Everyone who may see Orochia can watch it. |
| Unlock | You pay a price in credits to watch it. The price is shown. |
| Followers | Only the creator's approved followers. |
| Contacts | Only the creator's contacts. |
| Invited | Only people the creator invited. |
| Auction | Only the winner of its auction. See [Auctions](/docs/auctions). |
| Backers only | Only the backers of the challenge it was made for. See [Challenges](/docs/challenges). |
| 18+ | For adults. You need a signed-in account whose age Orochia has checked. |
| Sensitive content | The picture is veiled until you tap to reveal it. |

## Words about money

| Word | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| Credits | What you pay with. 1 credit is 1 US cent. See [Credits, tips and unlocks](/docs/credits). |
| Tip | An amount you send a creator to say thanks. |
| Held | Credits set aside while you lead an auction or back a challenge. They come back if you do not win or it is not delivered. |

## Words about collecting and sharing

- **Collection.** A set of videos you save under a name. You choose who can open it: everyone, followers, contacts, invited people, or only you. A collection never opens a video that the video's own rule keeps closed.
- **List.** A set of people you name, such as Close friends. Creators use lists to invite people to a video or a collection.
- **Contact.** Someone who accepted your request, and whom you accepted.
- **Follower.** Someone the creator approved.

## Words about challenges

- **Goal.** A creator sets a target. Fans pledge until the deadline.
- **Dare.** You ask one creator to make something.
- **Open call.** You ask any creator. Creators apply, you pick one.
- **Pledge.** Credits you put behind a challenge.

See [Challenges](/docs/challenges) for the rules.
