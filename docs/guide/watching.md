## What a video asks

The words below are defined once in the [Vocabulary](/docs/vocabulary).

Every video carries a label that says who can watch it.

| Label | Who can watch |
| :--- | :--- |
| Free | Everyone who may see Orochia |
| Followers | The creator's approved followers |
| Contacts | The creator's contacts |
| Unlock | People who unlocked it with credits |
| Invited | People the creator invited |
| Auction | Only the winner of its auction |
| Backers | Only the backers of the challenge it was made for |

If you cannot watch a video, the page tells you why and what to do. Adult videos also need a signed-in account whose age Orochia has checked. See [Safety and the 18+ rules](/docs/safety).

## Following a creator

1. Open the creator's page.
2. Choose **Follow**.
3. The creator approves their followers. Until they accept, your request is waiting.

Once the creator accepts you, their videos for followers open to you. You can unfollow at any time.

## Stories

Stories are short and last 24 hours. A ring around a creator's picture means there is a story you have not seen. Open it, tap right for the next one and left for the previous one.

## Contacts and messages

A contact is a two-way link: one person asks, the other accepts. A creator can share videos with their contacts only.

You decide who can send you messages in **Settings, Privacy**. Accounts you block cannot message you, follow you or ask to be your contact. You manage them in **Settings, Blocked accounts**.

## Unlocking and tipping

When a video asks for an unlock, you pay at least the amount it shows. See [Credits, tips and unlocks](/docs/credits).
