Messages are private conversations between people on Orochia. Open **Messages** from your account menu, or the **Messages** tab in the app.

## Start a conversation

Type a username, say hello. You decide who can write to you in **Settings, Privacy**: everyone with a verified account, or your contacts only. Accounts you block cannot write to you.

You can reply to a story from the story itself. The reply arrives as a message and the conversation shows which story it answers.

## Who sees that you are online

People you talk with, your contacts and your approved followers can see when you are online or when you were last seen. Nobody else can. In **Settings, Privacy**, turn off **Show when I'm online** and your circle sees you as offline, with no time. You will not see theirs either: nobody watches without being seen.

Under each message you sent, you see whether it was sent, delivered or seen.

## Groups

A group is a conversation for up to 50 people, you included.

1. In **Messages**, choose **New group**.
2. Name it and pick people from your contacts. A creator can also add their approved fans.
3. Choose **Create the group**. You are its owner.

As the owner you can:

- add people, up to the 50 places;
- remove a member;
- make an **invitation link**. Anyone with the link can join for a week, while places remain. You can cancel the links at any time. Share a link only with people you trust;
- rename the group, or close it. A closed group can still be read, but nobody can write in it.

Every member can **mute** the group for some hours (no notification and no badge) and **leave** it. If the owner leaves, the group goes to another member. To come back after leaving you need a new invitation.

A group has no public page. Someone who is not a member cannot find it or know it exists.

## Report a message

Open the actions of a message and choose **Report**. People review reports, the most serious first. The sender is not told who reported. A reviewer sees the reported message with the ten before and after it, never the rest of the conversation. See [Safety and the 18+ rules](/docs/safety).

## Notifications

A message rings your phone only when none of your Orochia screens is open. See [Notifications](/docs/notifications).
