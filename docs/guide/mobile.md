The Orochia app puts the feed, stories, auctions, challenges and your messages in your pocket. It is the same Orochia as the website, with the same rules and the same account.

## What you can do in the app

- Watch the feed and open stories.
- Watch a video you are allowed to see.
- Back a challenge or bid in an auction.
- Read and answer your messages.
- See your credits and your notifications.
- Create an account, or sign in to the one you have.

Some things stay on the website: buying credits, unlocking a video, and starting an auction or a challenge. The app never asks for your card. When you need one of these, it sends you to orochia.com.

## Get the app

**Android.** Install it from the releases page.

1. On your phone, open [the releases page](https://github.com/krizaka/orochia-mobile/releases).
2. Download the file ending in **.apk** from the latest release.
3. Open the downloaded file.
4. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps from this source, then go back.
5. Choose **Install**, then **Open**.

**iPhone.** Not yet. The iPhone app will come with TestFlight soon. Until then, use orochia.com in your browser.

## Your first launch

1. The app opens on the 18+ gate. Choose **I am 18 or older — enter**. If you are not, leave: it is one tap. The answer stays on your phone.
2. You see the feed. You can look around without an account.
3. To do more, open the **You** tab and sign in.

![The 18+ gate on a fresh install](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/site/docs/orochia/mobile/01-age-gate-dark.d726b8f556.webp "phone")

### Sign in

1. Open the **You** tab.
2. Type your e-mail or your username, then your password.
3. Choose **Sign in**.

![Sign in, with Create an account below](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/site/docs/orochia/mobile/16-sign-in-dark.21969a3622.webp "phone")

### Create an account

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Create an account**.
2. Fill in your e-mail, a username, a password of 10 characters or more and your date of birth.
3. Tick that you are 18 or older and that you accept the terms.
4. Open the e-mail we send you and follow the link. The app notices when you come back.

If you give a date of birth under 18, the app refuses it at once.

![Create an account](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/site/docs/orochia/mobile/10-sign-up-dark.300219226f.webp "phone")

## Watch stories and videos

1. On **Home**, tap a ring at the top to open a creator's stories.
2. Tap the right side for the next story and the left side for the previous one. Tap the cross to leave.
3. Tap a video in the feed to open it.
4. If a video asks for an unlock, the app shows its price and **Unlock on orochia.com**. Unlock it there, then come back.

![A story, full screen](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/site/docs/orochia/mobile/07-story-dark.a195f81935.webp "phone")

## Back a challenge

1. Open the **Challenges** tab.
2. Choose a goal. You see how much is raised, the time left and the top backers.
3. Choose an amount, then **Pledge**.
4. Your credits are held. They go to the creator when the video is delivered, and come back to you if it is not.

![A goal that is being funded](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/site/docs/orochia/mobile/17-challenge-dark.41942ef84a.webp "phone")

## Bid in an auction

1. Open the **Auctions** tab and choose an auction.
2. Pick a bid from the amounts offered.
3. Choose **Bid**. If you lead, the app says **You lead**.
4. If someone outbids you, your credits come back at once.

![An auction you lead](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/site/docs/orochia/mobile/18-auction-dark.aaae35aaa5.webp "phone")

## Your account

The **You** tab shows your credits and what is held in bids and pledges, your notifications, your public page and **Sign out**. Credits are added on the website.

![Your account, with credits and earnings](https://krizaka-assets.b-cdn.net/site/docs/orochia/mobile/05-account-earnings-dark.28ce17d1a1.webp "phone")

## When something does not work

### I get no notifications

1. Open **You**, then **Notification settings**, and check the kinds you want are on.
2. Open your phone's settings and allow notifications for Orochia.
3. Notifications to a closed app work on Android today. iPhone notifications come with the iPhone app.
4. A message does not ring the phone while another Orochia screen of yours is open. It shows there instead.

### I hear no sound

1. Check your phone is not on silent or Do Not Disturb.
2. In your phone's settings for Orochia, check the notification sound is on.

### I cannot sign in

1. Check your e-mail or username and your password. You can use either one to sign in.
2. If you just created the account, confirm your e-mail first. Use the link we sent you.
3. If the app says Orochia is out of reach, check your connection and pull the screen down to try again.
4. If you forgot your password, use **Forgot your password?** on orochia.com.
