## Adults only

Orochia is for people aged 18 or older. You confirm this when you sign up and give your date of birth, and the server checks your age from it. A box ticked in a browser opens nothing by itself.

Adult videos play, unlock, take bids and accept pledges only for a signed-in account whose age has been checked. If you are not, the page says so.

## What creators must do

Before a creator can publish, Orochia reviews records that prove they are an adult (18 U.S.C. § 2257). Each time they upload, they confirm that everyone shown is an adult, agreed to appear, and that the records are kept.

## Report something

If you see something wrong on a video or a story:

1. Open the video or the story.
2. Choose **Report**.
3. Pick a reason and add details if you can.
4. Choose **Send report**.

The reasons are: someone may be under 18, shared without consent, copyright, breaks the rules, and scam or deception. People review every report. Possible minors and content shared without consent come first, and a video reported as possibly involving a minor is removed at once while we review.

## Block someone

Blocked accounts cannot message you, follow you or ask to be your contact. Manage them in **Settings, Blocked accounts**.

## Copyright

If your work was used without your permission, you can report it with the Copyright reason or use the [DMCA page](/legal/dmca).
