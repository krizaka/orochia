There is one kind of account on Orochia. You join as a member. To publish, you open a creator space on the same account. You keep your followers, your credits, your purchases and your messages. Nobody signs up twice.

## The way to publishing

1. **Your account.** You are signed in, your e-mail is confirmed and your date of birth shows you are 18 or older.
2. **Open your creator space.** Choose **Open my creator space** on the creator page. Nothing is published and nothing is charged.
3. **Identity and age review.** The law (18 U.S.C. § 2257) asks for a record that proves everyone who appears is an adult. Orochia reviews it before you can publish. For now a person at Orochia does this by hand and writes to your e-mail address if a document is needed.
4. **Publish and get paid.** Once you are approved, you can upload videos and 24-hour stories, run auctions, set goals and receive tips.

## While you wait for approval

You can still watch, follow, tip, unlock and message. You can edit your profile (picture, cover, bio, links) and add where your earnings will be sent. You cannot upload yet, and you do not appear in Explore.

## After approval

- Upload videos and stories, and choose who can watch each one.
- Start auctions, set goals and answer dares.
- Receive tips on everything you publish.
- Show the verified badge on your profile.

## Rules for everything you publish

- Everyone who appears must be an adult and must have agreed to appear.
- You confirm this each time you upload.
- You cannot publish what you do not have the right to publish.

See [Safety and the 18+ rules](/docs/safety).

## Get paid

1. Add a payout method on the Earnings page. Orochia checks it like a bank would and shows it back to you only in part.
2. Your earnings build up as people tip, unlock, win auctions and back challenges you deliver.
3. When you have at least $20, you can ask for a payout.

Orochia keeps a platform fee from each payment. The share you keep is written on the creator page and on your Earnings page.
